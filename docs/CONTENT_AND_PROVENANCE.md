# Content and provenance policy

## Purpose

This repository is a terminology and game-design research index. It is not a rules archive.

## Source handling

Use official publisher pages, public quick-reference sheets, SRDs, official wikis, legally accessible PDFs,
or other clearly public rules resources where possible. Store the source URL and a short provenance note.

## Text handling

Do not paste substantial source text into the database. Record:
- the term;
- aliases where useful;
- a concise original paraphrase;
- the mechanical role;
- typical value or unit type;
- design distinctions;
- source provenance.

## Classification

`publication_audit` separates records into generic design concepts, historical/common terminology,
game-specific labels, and records needing review. A game-specific label can remain in the research database,
but should retain clear provenance.

## Trade marks and names

Game titles, publisher names and distinctive labels may be trade marks or otherwise protected identifiers.
They are used here for identification, attribution and comparative research. Their inclusion does not imply
ownership, affiliation or endorsement.

## Before a public release

Review records classified `game-specific label` or `review`, confirm source URLs, and check that definitions
remain original paraphrases rather than source wording.
